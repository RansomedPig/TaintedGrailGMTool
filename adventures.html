First Steps in Avalon (Quickstart)
Discover the setting and rules, then experience your first adventure in the lands of Avalon with premade Guardians. This quickstart contains a complete scenario, Sanguine Salt, in which you play survivors who land on Avalon's treacherous shores.
▶ Foreword

First Steps In Avalon is a quickstart for the role-playing game Tainted Grail: Song of a Dying World. Discover the setting and rules, then experience your first adventure in the lands of Avalon with premade Guardians.

The quickstart includes:

  • Introduction to the Setting — Tainted Grail is a twilight fantasy setting with a melancholic and corrupted reinterpretation of the Arthurian cycle and Celtic myths.
  • Rules Overview — Uses an adapted version of the Shadows of Esteren rules system. Intuitive and accessible, emphasis on roleplaying and moral development.
  • Premade Characters — Four simplified Character Sheets: Algren the dedicated bodyguard; Thane Jarna; Sheem the reckless navigator; and Velnort the clan chronicler.
  • First Song: Sanguine Salt — A complete scenario in which you play survivors landing on Avalon's treacherous shores.
▶ Introduction to the Setting

Tainted Grail is a twilight fantasy setting inspired by the Arthurian myth and Celtic legends. The Characters belong to one of the last surviving human communities after a terrible cataclysm, the Fall of Avalon, which occurred a century ago on the mysterious isle of Avalon.

The island was once home to the legendary King Arthur and his people, who fled a deadly epidemic known as the Red Death. However, their new home was no hospitable shelter: soon, humanity had to wrest its right to exist and come to terms with Avalon's endemic forces, such as the Wyrdness—a transformative magical phenomenon frequently occurring as a dense fog—and the Fore-dwellers—four-armed titans believed to be the island's original inhabitants.

Before Avalon

So much knowledge was lost in the exodus to Avalon. Upon leaving the Homelands, humanity renounced its roots. They followed King Arthur across the sea, himself guided by a disembodied voice and supernatural visions. Arthur and his own landed on a strange island. There was no Red Death to be found here: only a far more fearsome ancestral calamity.

The Fore-dwellers and the Conquest of Avalon

After landing on Avalon, humanity's ordeal was far from over. The island wasn't uninhabited and Man now had to contend with the Fore-dwellers: powerful, four-armed creatures encased in heavy armor. Thanks to treachery within their ranks and Arthur's valor, the giants lost their edge and were driven back to the heart of the island. Then began the construction of Camelot, the seat of royal power.

Merlin and the Menhirs

Merlin was Arthur's most trusted advisor and a great arcane theorist. He magically altered the standing stones that dot the island. The menhirs gained the power to repel the Wyrdness and weaken the Fore-dwellers. Over three centuries later, the menhirs' magic was extinguished and no one knows why. Avalon fell prey to chaos and the primordial Wyrdness.

Queen Morgaine and the Raven Lord

With the Wyrdness and the Fore-dwellers, the age-old hatred returned. Humanity went mad and the island descended into civil war. One century later, the island has been divided into many smaller kingdoms. In the northern reaches, Lord Mordred learned necromancy and made pacts with dark powers. Queen Morgaine governs Camelot with her council, pushing for the recolonization of the island.

The Wyrdness

The Wyrdness isn't just a thick, mile-wide fog that holds the people of Avalon hostage. It moves with the wind, but seems to have a consciousness of its own. Everything it swallows—plants, beasts, men—is spat back out: broken, distorted, maddened, altered. Only an insane person would venture into the Wyrdness without a Wyrdcandle or some protective talisman.

Avalon's Defenders

Mistfarers are explorers, guides and sentries. Knights are the only ones who can stand up against the Fore-dwellers. Wyrdhunters are zealot avengers, hunting down abominations that slay peasants and knights alike.

▶ Rules Overview

Ways

A Character's mental or physical skills aren't defined by ability scores. Instead, the five Ways determine their major personality traits:

  • Awareness — link with their environment, the wild and the unpredictable world
  • Combativeness — ardor and pugnacity, motivation and will to act
  • Conviction — fervor, commitment and beliefs
  • Creativity — imagination, inventiveness and resourcefulness
  • Reason — intellect, rationalization, capacity to learn

Each Way ranges from 1 to 5, with a different rating assigned to each.

Skills (Domains)

Skills are divided into 20 generic Domains. Each extends into Disciplines for specialization. Domain ratings max at 5; Disciplines range from 6 to 15.

Interpreting Skill Ratings: 0 = no knowledge; 1-2 = beginner; 3-5 = decent; 6-9 = professional; 10-12 = expert; 13-14 = master; 15 = living legend.

Resolution of Action

Roll 1D10 + Domain (or Discipline) + associated Way against a Difficulty Threshold.

Difficulty Thresholds: Easy 8 | Standard 11 | Complicated 14 | Difficult 17 | Very Difficult 20 | Exceptional 25 | Heroic 30 | Superhuman 35

Combat

Confrontations are brutal and merciless. The system emphasizes the danger of armed confrontation. Every battle can result in death. Players are urged to exercise caution and strategize.

Health & Sanity

Characters track Health conditions (Good, Okay -1, Bad -2, Critical -3, Agony) and Sanity via Mental Resistance rolls and Torment/Rout mechanics.

The Wyrdness

The Wyrdness manifests as magical mist with varying levels of intensity. Effects range from minor discomfort to deadly transformation. Wyrdcandles and menhirs provide protection.

▶ Sanguine Salt — Overture

The scenario Sanguine Salt is the introductory adventure. Players take on the role of survivors landing on Avalon's treacherous shores. The Overture sets the scene and introduces the Characters to their situation.

This Song is designed for 2 to 4 players using the premade characters provided. One participant takes on the role of the leader (game master) while the others play the Guardians.

▶ Stanza 1: An Eventful Arrival

The Characters arrive at a coastal settlement battered by recent storms. The community is on edge after losing contact with neighboring villages. The Guardians must establish themselves, gather information, and decide how to approach the growing tension among the settlers.

Key elements include meeting the local population, investigating rumors of disappearances, and navigating social dynamics between factions within the hamlet.

▶ Stanza 2: Avalonian Hospitality

The Characters delve deeper into the community's troubles. They discover that the settlement's problems are not merely mundane — the Wyrdness creeps ever closer, and not everyone is what they seem. Trust is a rare commodity in Avalon.

This stanza focuses on investigation, social interaction, and building alliances. The Characters must piece together clues while managing the suspicion directed at outsiders.

▶ Stanza 3: The Hour of the Wolves

The climax of the adventure. The threats converge and the Characters must make difficult choices under pressure. Combat is likely, but clever players may find alternative solutions. The Wyrdness plays a central role in the final confrontation.

This stanza tests all the skills the players have learned: combat, social interaction, exploration, and moral decision-making. Multiple outcomes are possible depending on the Characters' actions throughout the scenario.

▶ Epilogue

The aftermath of the Characters' choices. Depending on outcomes, the settlement may be saved, transformed, or abandoned. The epilogue provides hooks for continuing adventures and transitions into the full Tainted Grail campaign.

The Paths of Duty
A collection of scenarios for Tainted Grail: Song of a Dying World. Two adventures to start your epic journey as Guardians through the lands of Avalon: a solo Tale and a full introductory Song.
▶ Foreword

The Paths of Duty gives you two adventures to start your epic journey as Guardians through the lands of Avalon.

  • The Roots of Deadweald — A short choose-your-own-adventure Tale that teaches players and leaders about Tainted Grail with a step-by-step introduction. Play as Heane, a young woman traveling with her clan in their ultimate trek to find a safe, fertile land at the heart of Avalon.
  • The Price of Loyalty — A complete introductory scenario exploring the themes and landscapes of the Tainted Grail TTRPG. Play the loyal defenders of the Bedegraine March, exposed to danger by the disappearance of its lord.
▶ The Roots of Deadweald — Introduction

The Roots of Deadweald is a Tale, a short Choose-Your-Own-Adventure scenario lasting 30 to 45 minutes. This Tale is for all participants at the table, regardless of role. Players and leaders alike can embark upon this adventure and discover Heane's story, which serves as an introduction to the universe and game mechanics.

It can be played by individual participants, one by one, or in pairs. If you opt to work as a duo, one participant can be the narrator, reading the directions aloud, while the other plays Heane.

What You Need

  • A ten-sided die (D10)
  • Heane's Character Sheet (provided)
  • Paper and pencil for notes

Heane's Character

Heane is a courageous 15-year-old nomad shepherdess from the People of the Hinterlands. She journeys through the mist with her family and their clan on a quest for a safe haven. Her Ways: Combativeness 5, Creativity 2, Awareness 4, Conviction 3, Reason 1. She has the Mistwalker advantage.

▶ The Roots of Deadweald — Tale

For days now, you have been traveling in a convoy together with what remains of your clan. Recently driven from your homeland by the Wyrdness, your only option was to set off for the unknown, for a land of sanctuary: Vertbreuil, a thousand-year-old forest in the middle of the island of Avalon, protected from the mists by an impenetrable ring of trees.

Day after day, the mist and wild beasts have thinned your ranks. Your group now numbers only a dozen scrawny individuals. Your parents are at the head of the procession, armed with the last working ogham, a magical stone that can keep the Wyrdness at bay.

The Tale introduces key mechanics through play:

  • First Resolution Roll — Perception, Compassion, or Natural Environment to assess a stranger approaching your convoy
  • Combat: Initiative — Roll D10 + Speed to determine order of action
  • Combat: Actions — Move (6 yards) and perform an action each Round
  • Contested Actions — Stealth vs. pursuers' Perception
  • The Wyrdness — Encounter with the mist and its effects

Each resolution has success and failure paths that branch the narrative, teaching consequences of dice rolls while advancing Heane's story toward Deadweald.

▶ The Price of Loyalty — Introduction

The Price of Loyalty is a complete introductory scenario for Tainted Grail: Song of a Dying World. It explores the themes and landscapes of the setting through a self-contained adventure.

The Characters play loyal defenders of the Bedegraine March, a moor on the edge of Camelot's domain. The March has been exposed to danger by the disappearance of Lord Rhunon and the departure of the region's armed forces to seek him out. With the fate of the inhabitants in your hands, you must give them reason to hope again.

This Song is designed to be played over 2-3 sessions and features themes of loyalty, duty, survival, and the moral complexity of leadership in desperate times.

▶ The Price of Loyalty — Game Setting

The Bedegraine March is a remote territory on Camelot's frontier. Its lord, Rhunon, was a just ruler who earned the loyalty of his subjects through fairness and protection. The March contains several hamlets connected by dangerous roads that cut through areas of varying Wyrdness levels.

Key locations, NPCs, and factions are established here, along with the political situation that creates the adventure's central tension.

▶ The Price of Loyalty — Overture

The adventure opens with the Characters in their routine roles within the March's convoy system. Lord Rhunon has been missing for weeks, and the armed forces left to find him days ago. The Characters are among the few defenders remaining.

The Overture establishes the initial situation, introduces key NPCs, and presents the first challenges: maintaining order, protecting civilians, and deciding priorities with limited resources.

▶ Stanza 2: Unwanted Guests

Outsiders arrive in the March — refugees, opportunists, or something worse? The Characters must decide how to handle the newcomers while managing growing unrest among the locals. Resources are scarce and trust is in short supply.

This stanza emphasizes social challenges, investigation, and moral dilemmas. The Characters' choices here will shape the rest of the adventure and determine which allies and enemies they accumulate.

▶ Stanza 3: The Coils of the Wyrdness

The Wyrdness closes in on the March. The true threat reveals itself — a supernatural danger that tests the Characters' combat prowess, magical knowledge, and resolve. Previous choices come to fruition as allies prove their worth or betrayals are revealed.

This climactic stanza combines exploration of Wyrdness-affected terrain, confrontation with supernatural threats, and the culmination of the political tensions established earlier. Multiple paths to resolution exist depending on earlier decisions.

▶ Epilogue

The fate of the Bedegraine March is decided. Depending on the Characters' actions, the region may be stabilized under new leadership, fall to chaos, or find an unexpected path forward. Lord Rhunon's fate is revealed, and the Characters must reckon with the consequences of their loyalty and duty.

The epilogue provides experience rewards, Chronicle of Avalon integration points, and seeds for future adventures in the larger Tainted Grail campaign.
